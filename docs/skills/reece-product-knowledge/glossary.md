# Glossary — Reece Product Knowledge

**30-Day Price Guarantee** — unconfirmed — don't use. Old-brochure refund promise (within 30 days of contract, like-for-like on all 33 features); not in the canon Offer Ladder; separate from in-home pricing good for a full year (Ch 5, 8)
**Acts of God** — hurricanes, floods, earthquakes etc.; excluded from service pledge and glass breakage (Ch 5)
**Annealed glass** — standard untempered glass; upgraded to double tempered (Ch 4)
**Argon fill** — dense inert gas between panes that cuts heat transfer (Ch 2)
**Bay & Bow** — projecting multi-window units for traditional/Victorian homes (Ch 1)
**Cardinal Endur IG** — insulating glass unit; 0.20% failure over 20 years, 20-year factory warranty (Ch 2)
**Condensation** — water/frost formed when warm moist air meets a cooler surface (Ch 7)
**Condensation Resistance (CR)** — NFRC 1–100 rating; Tech 2000 DH = 59 (Ch 2)
**Conservation Glass** — dual-pane, argon, triple-silver low-E, stainless spacer glass package (Ch 2)
**Conservation Strength Glass** — glass claimed to "virtually stop glass breakage" (Ch 4)
**Constant force balance** — spring system holding a hung sash in place (Ch 1, Ch 4)
**CPE / TiO₂** — chlorinated polyethylene and titanium dioxide modifiers in the 2001 ZX PVC compound (Ch 4)
**Defect** — imperfection that impairs the product's utility (Ch 5)
**Double Lifetime warranty** — lifetime frame (vinyl) + glass coverage (Ch 4, Ch 5)
**Drop-in glazing** — glazing method for tighter fit and easier repair (Ch 4)
**ENERGY STAR** — US-government efficiency program; Conservation claims to exceed it in every state (Ch 2)
**Entry-Guard lock** — tamper-resistant heavy-duty sash lock (Ch 4)
**F.O.B. Dealer** — replacement glass shipped free to the dealer; Reece installs it free under the Free Service Pledge (Ch 5)
**Free Service Pledge** — free service for product life incl. labor, workmanship, materials; acts of God excluded (Ch 5)
**Fusion-welded miter** — corners melted together; 12-point welds (Ch 4)
**Glass Breakage — No Fault** — lifetime for original owner-occupant, 20 years for successor (Ch 5)
**Hurricane (laminated) glass** — two panes bonded by interlayer 3× thicker than a windshield's (Ch 3)
**HVHZ** — High Velocity Hurricane Zone (Miami-Dade, Broward); impact line approved (Ch 3)
**Insulated glass (IG) unit** — sealed multi-pane glass; lifetime warranty vs between-pane film/dust (Ch 2, Ch 5)
**Interlocking meeting rails** — rails that hook together; less air, harder forced entry (Ch 4)
**Intrahesive glazing** — glass and vinyl bonded together; stops bowing (Ch 4)
**Low-E3** — triple-silver low-emissivity coating (Ch 2, Ch 3)
**NFRC** — National Fenestration Rating Council; independent energy label (Ch 2)
**Non-prorated** — coverage value doesn't decline with age (Ch 5)
**Regency Plus Inc.** — manufacturer of Conservation™ Windows (Ch 5, Ch 8)
**Seal failure** — broken IG seal → fog between panes (Ch 7)
**Ship-Loc** — patented system keeping sash-to-frame spacing uniform (Ch 4)
**SHGC** — Solar Heat Gain Coefficient; lower = less heat gain (Ch 2)
**Single-hung** — window where only the bottom sash opens; offered by Reece, not in the source brochures, so no specs claimed (Ch 8)
**Single-pane** — the old uninsulated glass being replaced; NOT the single-hung style (Ch 2)
**Spectrally selective** — passes light, blocks heat and UV (Ch 2)
**STC** — Sound Transmission Class; 46 claimed (Ch 4)
**Tech 2000 Series 1712** — NFRC-labeled PVC double-hung with standard double glazing (U 0.29, SHGC 0.19) — not impact glass; never quote as an impact-window spec (Ch 2)
**Tilt-in sash** — sash tilts inward for cleaning from inside (Ch 1)
**Transfer (warranty)** — notify within 15 days of settlement, original owner signs (Ch 5)
**U-Factor** — heat-flow rate; lower = better insulation (Ch 2)
**UV / fading transmission** — Conservation Glass .05 UV, .43 fading (Ch 2)
**Visible Transmittance (VT)** — share of visible light through; 66% glass, 0.45 whole window (Ch 2)
**Warm-edge spacer** — low-conductivity spacer that stops edge cold/condensation (Ch 4)
**Weep system** — drainage path out of frames and tracks (Ch 6)
