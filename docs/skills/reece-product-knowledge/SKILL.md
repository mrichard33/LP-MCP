## Core Facts & Decision Rules

### Product line (ch01)
Conservation™ Windows by **Regency Plus Inc.**, custom-made vinyl, sold by Reece. Families (this brochure): **Double-Hung** (tilt-in), **Slider / Tilt-In Slider**, **Casement & Awning**, **Bay & Bow**, **Garden**, **Sliding Patio Door**; chart also lists Picture, Round Top, Hopper, Trapezoids. Colors: Euro-White, Solid Tan, White/Tan, White/Brown, Woodgrain/White. Grids (inside the glass): Colonial, Diamond, Prairie, Classic Double Prairie in white, tan, dark oak. **Single-Hung is also offered** — owner ruling, ch08; it is in none of the four PDFs, so say YES on availability and claim no specs for it. Pick by operation constraint → architecture → options.

### Conservation Glass & energy (ch02)
Dual-pane, **argon**, **triple-silver low-E** ("12-layer"), stainless spacer, supplied as Cardinal **Endur IG** (0.20% failure over 20 yrs).
- Center-of-glass: **U .29/.24 · SHGC .27 · VT 66% · UV .05** vs ordinary low-e U .35/.31, SHGC .72.
- **Stops 99% of UV** (protects carpet, drapes, furniture); thermal performance **equal to 5 panes**; **50% quieter** than ordinary insulated glass.
- **64% less heat gain** than ordinary glass; room-side glass **52°F at −20°F outside** (clear double: 37°F).
- NFRC label, **Tech 2000 Series 1712 Double Hung**: **U 0.29 · SHGC 0.19 · VT 0.45 · Air Leakage 0.1 · CR 59**. **These NFRC label values are for the Tech 2000 Series 1712 double-hung with standard double glazing — not laminated impact glass.** Never quote them as the spec for an impact window; impact units carry their own NFRC labels. The center-of-glass tables above are also for standard Conservation Glass.
- Claims to exceed ENERGY STAR in every state.
- Rule: compare competitors on **NFRC whole-window labels**; use center-of-glass tables to explain why. Don't mix the two. Never promise per-home savings.

### Hurricane & security glass (ch03)
Laminated: two panes + interlayer **3× thicker than a windshield's**. Tested: **several hits from a 9 lb 2×4 at 50 ft/s**. Impact line **approved for HVHZ incl. Miami-Dade**. Breaks but stays bonded → debris, burglary, noise, and Low-E3 energy benefits. Not every Conservation window is impact-rated.

The manufacturer's like-for-like feature list. Highlights: CPE/TiO₂-modified PVC; metal-alloy reinforced (**3× stronger than aluminum**); honeycomb extrusions; 12-point fusion-welded miters; laminated exterior glass (glazing bead inside); Entry-Guard locks; **double locks on windows over 20" wide**; interlocking meeting rails; intrahesive + drop-in glazing; warm-edge spacer; **STC 46**; tilt-in sashes; Ship-Loc; charcoal Teflon screen mesh; custom made; Conservation Glass® = **equal to 5 panes**, **guaranteed highest R value** (manufacturer's claim), **50% quieter**; **99% UV** stopped; Double Lifetime warranty = "best guarantee in the industry" (manufacturer's claim). ### Warranties & guarantees (ch05)
| Part | Term | Remedy / limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Vinyl** | Lifetime, while windows stay on property; transferable, non-prorated | Won't pit, corrode, crack; 100% parts & material |
| **Insulated glass** | Lifetime from manufacture; transferable | Film/dust **between** panes; room-side & pre-existing condensation excluded |
| **Free Service Pledge** | Life of product | **Free labor, workmanship and materials** on covered service; **acts of God excluded** |
| **Glass Breakage — No Fault** | Lifetime for original single-family owner-occupant; **20 yrs for successor** | Replacement glass free; **installation labor free under the Free Service Pledge**; removing the windows voids it; acts of God excluded |

- **Transfer**: notify within **15 days of settlement**; original owner signs card. Glass breakage transfer by certified mail.
- **Claim**: certified mail with install date, certificate number/date, proof of purchase. Unpaid balance can void.
- Dealer can't expand terms; no incidental/consequential damages; state law varies.
- **30-Day Price Guarantee (Reece) — unconfirmed — don't use.** From an old brochure; not in the canon Offer Ladder; it's a refund promise. Don't use in copy or bot replies until Mark confirms it's still offered. Separate from "in-home pricing good for a full year" (reece-story-canon).
- Coverage check: **part → exclusion → eligibility → remedy**.

### Care & maintenance (ch06)
Clean **yearly minimum, monthly if coastal**. Rinse first, mild soap + water, soft cloth, light pressure, one window at a time, out of direct sun. **Never**: hose/power washer, razor/putty knife/abrasive pad, petroleum, solvents, acids — they **may void the warranty**. Sliding door wheels: no lube (sealed). Hinges: mineral or plant oil. Keep tracks and weep systems clear; replace damaged weatherstrip. Don't lean on screens.

### Condensation (ch07)
**Windows don't cause condensation; humidity does.** New tight windows stop drafts that vented moisture, so condensation can increase. Room-side → reduce humidity (fans, open blinds, no humidifiers, dehumidifier, crack a window). **Between panes → seal failure → IG warranty.** Wall/ceiling damage or mold → heating & cooling contractor.
