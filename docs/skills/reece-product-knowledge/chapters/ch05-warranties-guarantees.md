# Chapter 5: Warranties & Guarantees

**Sources:** *Conservation Windows Double Lifetime Warranty* (scanned certificate set, Conservation Windows by Regency Plus Inc.), *Conservation Windows Warranty Information* (text version), brochure "Lifetime Warranties" page, Reece *30-Day Price Guarantee* (old brochure — unconfirmed, see below)

## Core Idea
The "Double Lifetime" warranty is a bundle of four manufacturer protections — vinyl frame, insulated glass, free service, and no-fault glass breakage. Each piece has its own duration, transfer rule and exclusions; answer warranty questions part by part.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The four-part warranty bundle** (Conservation Windows):
  1. **Lifetime Transferable Non-Prorated — Vinyl**
  2. **Lifetime Transferable Non-Prorated — Insulated Glass**
  3. **Free Service Pledge for Lifetime Guarantee**
  4. **Glass Breakage — No Fault** (Lifetime Non-Prorated Transferable Limited Warranty)
- **"Double Lifetime"** = frame + glass lifetime coverage (features chart #26).
- **15-day transfer rule**: warranty follows a home sale only if the owner acts within 15 days.
- **Reece 30-Day Price Guarantee — unconfirmed — don't use** (see the section below).

## Reference Tables

### Coverage matrix
| Part | Covers | Duration | Transfer | Remedy | Key exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vinyl** | Defects in vinyl; main & sash frames won't pit, corrode, crack under normal use | Lifetime from installation, "as long as the windows remain part of the property" | Yes | Repair/replace free; **100% of material & replacement parts** | "Defect" = imperfection that impairs product utility |
| **Insulated glass** | Material obstruction of vision from film or dust **between** the panes | Lifetime from date of **manufacture** | Yes | Repair/replace (brochure: free of charge) | Temporary condensation from temperature/humidity (baths, kitchens); **no warranty on pre-existing condensation or its control** |
| **Free Service Pledge** | Free service for the life of the product — **labor, workmanship and materials** | Life of product | Follows the product | **Free labor** on every covered repair or replacement | **Acts of God** (hurricanes, floods, earthquakes, etc.) |
| **Glass breakage — No Fault** | Glass breakage, any cause (no fault) | Original owner: lifetime while they own/reside (**single-family, owner-occupied**); successor: **20 years** non-prorated | Yes (converts to 20-yr) | Replacement glass free (manufacturer ships it F.O.B. Dealer); **installation labor free under the Free Service Pledge**; replaced glass keeps remaining term | Removal of windows voids it; acts of God (hurricanes, floods, earthquakes) |

**How the two combine:** the glass-breakage certificate says the *manufacturer* won't pay removal/reinstall costs. The Free Service Pledge covers that labor. **To the customer, a covered glass-breakage claim = free glass + free labor.**

### Transfer & claims procedure
| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Registration | Warranty card completed by purchaser and **signed by the dealer at installation** |
| On sale | Detach card, send to nearest Conservation Windows with new owner's name, effective date, **original owner's signature** (original owner relinquishes rights) |
| Deadline | Notify within **15 days of settlement**; delays from strikes/labor disputes at Conservation/factory extend it |
| Glass breakage transfer | New owner sends name + property address by **certified mail** within 15 days of becoming owner |
| Filing a claim | Written claim by **certified mail** stating installation date, certificate date & number, plus **proof of purchase** |
| Void risk | Unpaid balance owed to Conservation Windows "could cause" warranty to be null and void |

### Warranty card fields (for ops/CRM)
Transfer card: Date of Installation · Style/Model(s) · Dealer Name · Property Owner · Address/City/State/Zip · Conservation Windows Order Number · Dealer Signature · Transfer: New Owner, Date of Transfer, Original Owner Signature.
Certificate: Owners · Issue Date · Model/Style · Authorized Signature. Glass breakage certificate: Purchaser, Phone, Date of Contract, Products Purchased, Transferee, **Certificate Number**.

### Legal limits
Valid where permitted by state law; warranties are exclusive remedies in lieu of all others; **no liability for incidental or consequential damages**; **dealer cannot obligate Conservation Windows** beyond the certificate terms; state rights may vary.

## Key Concepts
- **Non-prorated**: coverage value doesn't shrink over time.
- **Transferable**: passes to the next owner if the transfer procedure is followed.
- **F.O.B. Dealer**: manufacturer ships the glass to the dealer free; Reece installs it at no charge under the Free Service Pledge.
- **Acts of God**: natural disasters — excluded from service pledge and glass breakage.
- **Proof of purchase / certificate number**: required for any claim.

## Mental Models
- Answer "Is X covered?" with three checks: **which part** → **is the cause excluded** (act of God, pre-existing condensation, window removal) → **is the owner eligible** (original vs successor, transfer filed in 15 days, balance paid).
- Think of glass breakage as **two warranties**: lifetime for the original owner-occupant, 20 years for the next owner.

## Anti-patterns
- **"Covers hurricane damage"**: acts of God are explicitly excluded.
- **"Lifetime for every owner"**: glass breakage becomes 20 years after transfer.
- **"Transfers automatically"**: requires notice within 15 days of settlement.
- **"Warranty covers condensation"**: only condensation/film *between* panes (seal failure); room-side condensation is excluded.
- **Quoting the brochure's glass-breakage summary**: it says "Non-Transferable" and then "transferable" in the same paragraph. The certificate (Transferable) governs.
- **Charging labor on a covered claim**: the Free Service Pledge makes labor, workmanship and materials free for the life of the product. Only acts of God are excluded.

## Worked Example
Customer: "My neighbor's kid threw a ball through my window, and I bought the house two years ago from the original owner. Covered?"
1. Part → Glass Breakage, No Fault (cause doesn't matter).
2. Exclusions → not an act of God; windows not removed. ✔
3. Eligibility → successor owner: covered for **20 years** only if the transfer was sent by certified mail within 15 days of becoming owner. If not filed, coverage is doubtful.
4. Remedy → replacement glass free; claim by certified mail with installation date, certificate number/date, proof of purchase.
5. Labor → free under the Free Service Pledge. Total cost to the customer: $0.

## Key Takeaways
1. Four parts: vinyl, insulated glass, free service, no-fault glass breakage.
2. Vinyl & IG: lifetime, transferable, non-prorated, 100% parts & material.
3. Glass breakage: lifetime for original owner-occupant, 20 years for successor; free glass + free labor (via the Free Service Pledge).
4. Transfer within 15 days of settlement; claims by certified mail with certificate number and proof of purchase.
5. Acts of God excluded; pre-existing/room-side condensation excluded; removal voids glass coverage.
6. 30-Day Price Guarantee: unconfirmed — don't use until Mark confirms.
