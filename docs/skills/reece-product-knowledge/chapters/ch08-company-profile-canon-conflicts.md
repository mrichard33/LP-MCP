## Company Facts
- **HQ: St. Petersburg (St. Pete office), FL.** License **CGC#1507607**.
- Started by **Michael Reece Sr. in 1972**; now run by his son **Randy Reece**, who grew up in the business.
- Family-business philosophy extends to employees and customers. Flywheel: treat employees well → they treat customers well → repeat business and referrals. "Everyone wins."
- Mission: **complete customer satisfaction** through superior replacement windows and doors installed by experienced installers; background checks and drug testing for everyone sent to a home.
- Manufacturer: **Conservation™ Windows by Regency Plus Inc.** Tagline on the warranty cover: "America's Most Trusted Name in Windows…" — the manufacturer's claim; never state it in Randy's voice as Reece's own.
- Reece logo: "Reece Windows & Doors — EST 1972."
