# Chapter 6: Care & Maintenance

**Source:** *Care & Maintenance Instructions*, ©2024 Reece Windows & Doors

## Core Idea
Proper, gentle cleaning on a set schedule extends product life, prevents costly repairs, and keeps the warranty intact. Certain tools and chemicals can damage products **and may void the warranty**.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Cleaning frequency rule**: at minimum **once a year**; **coastal homes at least once a month**; more often if the environment demands.
- **Important Precautions (never use)**:
  - Power washer or garden hose
  - Metal tools or sharp objects — razor blades, putty knives, abrasive pads
  - Petroleum-based products, abrasive or caustic cleaners/solvents, hydrochloric/phosphoric acid
- **Essential Best Practices**:
  - Light pressure — excessive scrubbing scratches.
  - Clean and rinse each window/door **individually** so cleaners don't sit on surfaces.
  - Clean **aluminum frames** very carefully after installation — highly susceptible to corrosion from construction-site chemicals.
  - Don't lean on screens — they are not fall protection.

## Reference Tables

### Cleaning procedures
| Component | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Glass** | 1) Rinse with clean water to remove grit (no hose/power washer) · 2) Apply soft water-saturated cloth or pre-mixed glass cleaner · 3) Follow cleaner's instructions; rinse and dry run-down quickly · 4) Rinse thoroughly to avoid film. **Tip:** clean in cooler temps, out of direct sun. Wash frequently to prevent stains. |
| **Frames** | 1) Rinse with clean water · 2) Clean interior & exterior with mild soap and water — spot-test first · 3) Keep **track systems clear** for storm drainage |
| **Screens** | 1) Remove carefully onto clean flat surface · 2) Soft brush + mild soap/water to remove dirt, salt, deposits; avoid puncturing · 3) Reinstall only when completely dry |
| **Hardware** | Sliding door wheels: **no lubrication** (sealed ball-bearing housing) · Hinges, thumb turns, moving parts: light oil — mineral oil or plant oil (hemp, olive, grapeseed) · Weatherstrip: damp cloth or mild soap/water, rinse & dry; **replace promptly if damaged** · Weep systems: inspect & clean routinely · Tracks & sills: damp cloth or vacuum |

## Key Concepts
- **Weep system**: drainage holes/channels that let water out of the frame and tracks.
- **Weatherstrip**: seal between sash and frame; damaged strip = air/water leaks.
- **Track system**: the channel a slider or door runs in; debris blocks storm drainage.
- **Coastal environment**: salt accelerates corrosion — drives monthly cleaning.

## Mental Models
- Think **"rinse first, then clean"** — grit is what scratches.
- Use the tool test: if it's metal, abrasive, pressurized or petroleum-based, it doesn't touch the product.
- For Florida homes, default to the **coastal (monthly)** schedule.

## Anti-patterns
- **Hosing or pressure-washing windows** — damage and possible warranty void.
- **Scraping with razor blades** (e.g., removing stickers/paint).
- **Lubricating sliding-door wheels** — they're sealed.
- **Petroleum lubricants** (e.g., WD-40-type products) on hardware — guide calls for mineral or plant-based oil.
- **Cleaning in direct sun** — cleaner dries into streaks.

## Worked Example
Homeowner on the Gulf coast: "What's my maintenance routine?"
- **Monthly** (coastal): rinse glass with a bucket and clean water, clean with soft cloth + glass cleaner in the shade; wipe frames with mild soap/water; vacuum tracks and sills; check weep holes are clear.
- **Every few months:** remove screens, soft-brush with mild soap/water to remove salt, dry fully, reinstall.
- **As needed:** a drop of mineral or olive oil on hinges and thumb turns; inspect weatherstrip and replace if damaged.
- **Never:** hose, pressure washer, razor, abrasive pad, solvents, acids.

## Key Takeaways
1. Clean at least yearly; monthly on the coast.
2. Rinse first, light pressure, one window at a time, mild soap and water.
3. Never hose, pressure-wash, scrape, or use petroleum/abrasive/acid cleaners — they may void the warranty.
4. Keep tracks and weep systems clear for storm drainage.
5. Sliding door wheels need no oil; hinges get light mineral/plant oil.
