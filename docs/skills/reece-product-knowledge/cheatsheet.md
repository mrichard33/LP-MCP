# Cheatsheet — Reece Product Knowledge

## Decision rules
- **If** a product stat is headed for customer copy → **use it as written**; manufacturer stats in this skill are the approved source of truth. **Product specs only.** For outcome/insurance/savings stats, use the five public references in reece-story-canon; never turn a spec into a savings, premium or claim outcome.
- **If** a covered repair or glass replacement needs labor → **free** under the Free Service Pledge.
- **If** someone asks "does the warranty cover hurricane damage?" → **No** — acts of God are excluded from the service pledge and glass breakage.
- **If** fog is **between** panes → seal failure → IG warranty claim. **If** on the room side → humidity, not a defect.
- **If** the home sold → warranty transfers **only** with notice within **15 days of settlement**; glass breakage drops to **20 years**.
- **If** comparing to a competitor → use **NFRC whole-window labels**, not center-of-glass tables.
- **If** someone asks about a price guarantee → the **30-Day Price Guarantee is unconfirmed — don't use** (old brochure; not in the canon Offer Ladder; a refund promise). Don't mention it in copy or bot replies until Mark confirms. It is separate from "in-home pricing good for a full year."
- **If** quoting NFRC numbers → U 0.29 / SHGC 0.19 are for the **Tech 2000 Series 1712 double-hung with standard double glazing, not impact glass**. Impact units carry their own NFRC labels.
- **If** a superlative ("guaranteed highest R value," "best guarantee in the industry," "America's Most Trusted Name") → attribute it to the manufacturer, never to Randy/Reece.
- **If** the question is about entry doors, other brands or roofing → not covered here; don't apply Conservation specs.
- **If** the home is coastal → clean **monthly**; otherwise at least **yearly**.
- **If** describing impact glass → "tested" / "approved for HVHZ incl. Miami-Dade" — **never** "hurricane-proof."

## Warranty at a glance
| Part | Term | Transfers? | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vinyl frame & sash | Lifetime (while on property) | Yes | 100% parts & material |
| Insulated glass | Lifetime from manufacture | Yes | Room-side/pre-existing condensation excluded |
| Free Service Pledge | Life of product | Follows product | **Free labor**, workmanship, materials; acts of God excluded |
| Glass breakage (no fault) | Life (original owner-occupant) / 20 yr (successor) | Yes | Free glass + free labor (pledge); removal voids; acts of God excluded |

Claim = certified mail + install date + certificate no./date + proof of purchase. Unpaid balance can void.

## Tells & smells
- "Employed in-house" / "Reece Builders" in a draft → lifted from old collateral; fix to canon wording.
- "Covers everything forever" → wrong; four parts, different terms, acts of God out.
- "95% UV" in a draft → use 99%.
- Single-hung treated as single-pane → different things entirely. Single-hung is a STYLE we offer (ch08); single-pane is the old GLASS we replace (ch02 tables). Never answer a style question with a glass stat.
- "We don't offer single-hung," or a styles list that omits it → wrong; it is offered (ch08). Leaving it out of a list of what we sell reads as a no.
- Labor charged on a covered claim → wrong; Free Service Pledge.
- SHGC 0.19 and .27 used interchangeably → whole-window vs center-of-glass mix-up (0.19 is the Tech 2000 double-hung label with standard glazing, not impact).
- NFRC 0.29/0.19 quoted for an impact window → wrong product (those are the Tech 2000 double-hung, standard glazing).
- "30-Day Price Guarantee" in a draft or bot reply → unconfirmed — don't use.
- HQ given as Ft. Lauderdale → outdated; HQ is St. Petersburg (St. Pete office).
- Brochure glass-breakage blurb says "Non-Transferable" → typo; certificate says Transferable.
