1 · Lead quality — they close well, but they buy small

On appointment-dated volume (137 Sales Efficiency By Mode), MyHomePros beats the house on every conversion step: net-issue 88% vs 70% company, close 40% STD vs 32%, on-time-demo 24% vs 17%. The defect is job size. Average sold job is $17,816 against a company average of $25,166 — 29% below — and NSLI is $1,489 vs $1,923. This is direct evidence for Brad's home-value thesis from Sep 9: the leads convert, the homes are too cheap to carry the product. Duplicates are not a problem — only 3 repeat phones inside the cohort and 21 of 413 (5.1%) already in-house from another source within 90 days.
